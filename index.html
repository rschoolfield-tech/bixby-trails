App, offline & privacy

On iPhone, open the hosted link in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App if shown, then Add. On Android, use your browser’s Install app / Add to Home screen.

After the first successful online visit, the hosted app saves its trail text and app files for offline browsing. Maps, directions, official updates and uncached photos need internet. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; keep a favorites backup. Trail notices are dated, not live; check the official links before leaving.

No login, tracking scripts, paid APIs or subscription are built into this app. Favorites and search preferences stay in this browser. Online photos load from external sites. Sharing sends only the app link, never your location or favorites.
